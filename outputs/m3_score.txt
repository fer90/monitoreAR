  Codes 30–35 within 20 km of the hot pixel hold the candidate back.
  Codes 10–15 are written in the log and do not hold it back: the second
  Flight 11 scan is itself code 10. A missing fire file stays in the log
  as fire_file=unavailable.
  The 98th percentile is logged and is not the score. On the hot scans it
  rose by about 5 K, which is under this threshold.
