Limits visible in this comparison
  The quiet mesoscale sample is 12 days after the launch, because the
  sectors were tasked elsewhere on the adjacent evenings. The Sun was
  2.5 deg closer to the horizon.
  The fire is a 5-minute CONUS pixel. The launch is a 1-minute mesoscale
  pixel. Satellite zenith is similar. The fire pixel is in darkness
  (solar zenith 95.6 deg) while the pad is still in daylight
  (solar zenith 82.1 deg), so the backgrounds are not the same
  physical mixture. Band 7 at the pad still includes reflected sunlight.
  A 346 K brightness temperature is the average of one pixel, not the
  flame temperature. The fire product's own temperature is a different
  quantity and is printed separately.
  The 20 km neighbourhood follows a hot pixel that moves. It also
  follows any other warm pixel inside that radius.
