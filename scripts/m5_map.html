monitoreAR

GOES-East ABI band 7 candidates for Starbase Pad 1. The points are hot pixels from the score stored in SQLite. The open marker is the pad.

Limits of this view

  • At Pad 1 a band-7 pixel is about 2.3 km east–west and 2.5 km north–south. The temperature is the Planck temperature of the average radiance in that pixel.
  • The engines are hotter and smaller than the pixel. Flight 11 peaked near 346 K. The radiance ceiling on those files is about 411 K, and those pixels were not saturated.
  • When one mesoscale sector contains the pad, the revisit is about 60 seconds and the scan itself lasts about 7 seconds. If neither sector contains the pad, that minute is a coverage gap. CONUS and full-disk scans are left unscored.
  • The score is the hottest pixel within 20 km minus the median of that maximum on the previous covering scans. The threshold is 20 K, chosen from the Flight 11 case study.
  • The stored coordinate is the hot pixel on the ellipsoid. The second Flight 11 pixel is about 10 km from the pad. This page does not correct parallax and does not draw a trajectory.
  • Small launch vehicles are often invisible at this resolution.
  • The imagery is NOAA GOES-19 ABI Level 1b, from the public bucket. monitoreAR is not a NOAA service and not an operational alert.
  • Each crop uses one colour scale, 283.43 K to 350 K, the scale of the Flight 11 figure.
